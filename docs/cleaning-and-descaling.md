# Cleaning and descaling

Regular cleaning keeps shots tasting right and protects the boiler.

## Daily

- Backflush the group head with water.
- Empty and rinse the drip tray.
- Wipe and purge the steam wand after every use.

## Weekly

Soak the portafilter and baskets in hot water with one cleaning tablet for
20 minutes, then rinse them well.

## Every 3 months

Descale every 3 months, or sooner when the descale light turns amber.

1. Empty the water tank and dissolve one descaling sachet in 1 L of water.
2. Hold the **power** and **steam** buttons together for 3 seconds. The ring
   light blinks amber while the descale programme runs (about 20 minutes).
3. When the light turns white, rinse the tank, fill it with fresh water and
   run the programme once more to flush it.

If the programme is interrupted, the descale light stays amber. Run the
programme again from the start.

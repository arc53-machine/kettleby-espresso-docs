# Warranty and returns

## Warranty

The Espresso One has a **2-year limited warranty** covering the boiler, pump
and electronics. Wear parts (gaskets, shower screen and baskets) are covered
for **6 months**.

The warranty does not cover damage from skipped descaling, unfiltered hard
water or accidental drops.

## Returns

You can return an unused machine within **30 days of delivery** for a full
refund.

## Making a claim

Send your order number and a short video of the fault to support through the
customer portal. We reply within two working days.

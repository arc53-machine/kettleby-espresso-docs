# Getting started with the Espresso One

## What's in the box

- Espresso One machine with a 1.8 L water tank
- 58 mm portafilter with single and double baskets
- Stainless steel milk jug (350 ml)
- Tamper, cleaning brush and two descaling sachets

## First-time setup

1. Rinse the water tank and fill it with filtered water up to the MAX line.
2. Plug the machine in and press the power button. The ring light pulses while
   the boiler heats, which takes about 90 seconds.
3. Run two empty shots through the group head to flush the system before your
   first coffee.

## Grind and dose

| Drink           | Dose | Yield | Time    |
| --------------- | ---- | ----- | ------- |
| Single espresso | 9 g  | 18 g  | 25–30 s |
| Double espresso | 18 g | 36 g  | 25–30 s |
| Ristretto       | 18 g | 25 g  | 20–25 s |

If a double shot runs faster than 20 seconds, grind finer. If it runs slower
than 35 seconds, grind coarser.

## Steaming milk

Purge the steam wand for two seconds, place the tip just below the milk surface
and open the steam knob fully. Stop at 60–65 °C. Wipe and purge the wand
straight after every use.
